Frozen target and limits

Target: the captured specimen identified by data/provenance.json, plus the user’s explicit classification of its purpose. Evidence review cutoff: October 13, 2026. Units of review: 24 selected propositions, not every assertion in the approximately 7,000-word text. Status counts are counts of selected propositions, not a random-sample hallucination rate.

The supplied EFMW Zoo analysis rules require frozen inputs, explicit transformations, independent evidence, proof obligations, and evidence-separated conclusions. A complete versioned animal-operation registry was not supplied for this task. Accordingly the operations below are named descriptive checks; no new animal definitions or claim of a complete all-animal run is introduced.

Evidence statuses

  • Demonstrated: supported within the narrow stated scope by directly available evidence. This does not imply a universal theorem.
  • Supported but incomplete: a core component has support, with a material qualification or missing step.
  • Hypothesis/postulate: a proposed model or scenario, not established by the supplied evidence.
  • Unresolved: insufficient evidence in this review to establish or settle the proposition.
  • Rejected: the stated claim, proof, inference, or claimed calculation fails the specified check. Read the finding to see which is rejected; it does not necessarily mean the opposite historical proposition has been proved.

Independence audit

Multiple paragraphs repeating the same accusation are not independent evidence. Multiple pages from the same investigation may share evidence and assumptions. The route check uses a commission transcription of newspaper descriptions rather than an independent inspection of the original newspapers. The secondary 2026 status update and NARA release page are related institutional sources. This package therefore makes no claim of a specified number of independent replications.

Selecting only secrets known to have been exposed makes eventual exposure a condition of entry into the sample. Reclassifying all contrary evidence as suppression prevents falsification. Neither step can serve as a legitimate probability estimate.

Survivors and rejected branches

The narrow existence and date of Executive Order 11110 survive. NSAM 263’s withdrawal planning survives with qualifications. Official records support legitimate scrutiny of withheld information and investigation quality. These survivors do not identify the alleged joint operation. Source existence is not operational culpability; benefit is not causation; numerical precision is not calibration.

Rejected branches include treating title and format as provenance, treating exposure-selected examples as a universal law, inferring a named conspiracy from the existence of secrecy, and treating ordinary document release as automatic confirmation of a specific assassination narrative.

Minimal next test

Freeze one claim and a primary-source identifier before additional interpretation. Success requires an independently accessible, authenticated source that supports the specific proposition without importing the conclusion. Failure includes an unverifiable source or a record that does not support the proposition. A failed source test leaves an historical question unresolved unless contrary evidence separately rejects it. See EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md for the prospective model-evaluation design.
