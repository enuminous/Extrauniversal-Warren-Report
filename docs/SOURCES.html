Project home · Claim register

Sources checked October 13, 2026. Links identify records used in this bounded review. Summaries are scoped paraphrases, not authenticated reproductions of entire collections. Source presence does not independently validate every statement in an official record.

S01

Warren Commission — Introduction

Publisher: U.S. National Archives. Type: institutional archive. Date: 2025-02-28. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Commission membership and report transfer paragraphs

Used for: Earl Warren chaired the commission; Allen Dulles was a member. The report was submitted September 24, 1964.

Limits: Membership identifies formal roles; it does not by itself settle informal influence or investigative adequacy. The page warns that some collection information may be outdated.

S02

Warren Commission Report, Chapter 2

Publisher: Warren Commission / U.S. National Archives. Type: primary investigation. Date: 1964. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Selection of motorcade route; printed pages 32–40; November 19 newspaper descriptions

Used for: Contemporaneous route descriptions reproduced in the report already included the Houston–Elm turns.

Limits: The route excerpts were inspected in the commission transcription, not independently against physical newspaper originals.

S03

Executive Order 11110

Publisher: John F. Kennedy / American Presidency Project. Type: primary executive order. Date: 1963-06-04. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Section 1(a), amendment to Executive Order 10289

Used for: Delegation of existing presidential authority concerning silver certificates; the text does not abolish or replace the Federal Reserve.

Limits: The order is evidence of its provisions, not of alleged motives for an assassination.

S04

National Security Action Memorandum No. 263

Publisher: Office of the Historian, U.S. Department of State. Type: primary policy memorandum. Date: 1963-10-11. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Document 194, paragraph on 1,000 personnel; signature McGeorge Bundy

Used for: Records presidential approval of recommendations and plans to withdraw 1,000 military personnel by the end of 1963.

Limits: This does not establish the counterfactual claim that Kennedy certainly would have ended all U.S. involvement.

S05

National Security Action Memorandum No. 273

Publisher: Office of the Historian, U.S. Department of State. Type: primary policy memorandum. Date: 1963-11-26. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Document 331, paragraph 2

Used for: Retains the withdrawal objectives stated in the October 2, 1963 White House statement.

Limits: A statement four days after the assassination does not describe all of Johnson’s later Vietnam policy.

S06

House Select Committee on Assassinations — Summary of Findings

Publisher: U.S. House of Representatives / U.S. National Archives. Type: primary congressional investigation. Date: 1979. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Findings I.A–D, especially fatal-shot attribution, probable conspiracy, and investigative inadequacy

Used for: The committee reported probable conspiracy but attributed the fatal shot to Oswald and did not identify the other gunman.

Limits: These are the committee’s findings, not an independent reanalysis here. The acoustic foundation was later rejected by the NRC; read S08 with this source.

S07

House Select Committee on Assassinations — Introduction

Publisher: U.S. House of Representatives / U.S. National Archives. Type: primary congressional investigation. Date: 1979. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Opening account of Church Committee findings; printed page 9

Used for: Documents that the CIA withheld information about U.S. plots against Fidel Castro from the Warren Commission.

Limits: Withholding that information does not establish who ordered Kennedy’s assassination.

S08

Report of the Committee on Ballistic Acoustics

Publisher: National Research Council. Type: scientific committee report. Date: 1982. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Summary, pages 1–2; DOI 10.17226/10264; accessible text at https://nap.nationalacademies.org/skim.php?act=nap&chap=1-2&record_id=10264

Used for: Rejected the acoustic basis for the asserted grassy-knoll shot and its 95% probability.

Limits: Rejecting one acoustic analysis is not a proof that every conceivable conspiracy is impossible.

S12

Gunshot-wound dynamics model for John F. Kennedy assassination

Publisher: Nicholas R. Nalli, Heliyon. Type: primary research abstract. Date: 2018-04-30. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Abstract; DOI 10.1016/j.heliyon.2018.e00603; linked corrigendum 10.1016/j.heliyon.2018.e00831

Used for: Presents a model in which the observed motion is consistent with an impact from behind, including delayed recoil and neuromuscular forces.

Limits: An abstract-level check of a model, not a new forensic reconstruction or proof of all assumptions. PubMed links an October 2018 corrigendum; use corrected material for numerical replication.
