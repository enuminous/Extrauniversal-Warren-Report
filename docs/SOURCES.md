# Source register

[Project home](../README.md) · [Claim register](CLAIM_REGISTER.md)

Sources checked October 13, 2026. Links identify records used in this bounded review. Summaries are scoped paraphrases, not authenticated reproductions of entire collections. Source presence does not independently validate every statement in an official record.

## S01

**[Warren Commission — Introduction](https://www.archives.gov/research/jfk/warren-commission-report/intro)**

Publisher: U.S. National Archives. Type: institutional archive. Date: 2025-02-28. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Commission membership and report transfer paragraphs

Used for: Earl Warren chaired the commission; Allen Dulles was a member. The report was submitted September 24, 1964.

Limits: Membership identifies formal roles; it does not by itself settle informal influence or investigative adequacy. The page warns that some collection information may be outdated.

## S06

**[House Select Committee on Assassinations — Summary of Findings](https://www.archives.gov/research/jfk/select-committee-report/summary.html)**

Publisher: U.S. House of Representatives / U.S. National Archives. Type: primary congressional investigation. Date: 1979. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Findings I.A–D, especially fatal-shot attribution, probable conspiracy, and investigative inadequacy

Used for: The committee reported probable conspiracy but attributed the fatal shot to Oswald and did not identify the other gunman.

Limits: These are the committee’s findings, not an independent reanalysis here. The acoustic foundation was later rejected by the NRC; read S08 with this source.

## S08

**[Report of the Committee on Ballistic Acoustics](https://www.nationalacademies.org/publications/10264)**

Publisher: National Research Council. Type: scientific committee report. Date: 1982. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Summary, pages 1–2; DOI 10.17226/10264; accessible text at https://nap.nationalacademies.org/skim.php?act=nap&chap=1-2&record_id=10264

Used for: Rejected the acoustic basis for the asserted grassy-knoll shot and its 95% probability.

Limits: Rejecting one acoustic analysis is not a proof that every conceivable conspiracy is impossible.

## S12

**[Gunshot-wound dynamics model for John F. Kennedy assassination](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29736430/)**

Publisher: Nicholas R. Nalli, Heliyon. Type: primary research abstract. Date: 2018-04-30. Accessed: 2026-10-13.

Locator: Abstract; DOI 10.1016/j.heliyon.2018.e00603; linked corrigendum 10.1016/j.heliyon.2018.e00831

Used for: Presents a model in which the observed motion is consistent with an impact from behind, including delayed recoil and neuromuscular forces.

Limits: An abstract-level check of a model, not a new forensic reconstruction or proof of all assumptions. PubMed links an October 2018 corrigendum; use corrected material for numerical replication.
